Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 17, 2026

1. Data We Collect

To provide our expense tracking services, we collect information that you provide directly to us, including your email address, password (encrypted), and the financial transactions you log (amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions).

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the collected data solely to operate and improve the Spendly service. This includes managing your account, generating your spending reports, and providing a personalized financial overview. We do not sell or rent your personal data to third parties.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely in our database. We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, or destruction. However, please remember that no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not share your data with third-party services unless required by law or if you explicitly authorize such integration in the future. Any third-party plugins or services used for site analytics may collect anonymous usage data.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please contact us at privacy@spendly.example.com.
